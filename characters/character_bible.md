# Character Bible – Strict Consistency Guide

## 1. Mayank Sharma (Protagonist)

**Age**: 28  
**Occupation**: Senior Brand Consultant in a big MNC  
**Package**: ~5 Lakh/month  

### Physical Appearance (Lock these details)
- Face shape: Oval, sharp jawline, high cheekbones
- Eyes: Dark brown, intense, initially slightly narrowed/arrogant
- Eyebrows: Well-shaped, slightly raised in superiority
- Nose: Straight, refined
- Hair: Short, stylish, side-parted black hair, always perfect in ego phase
- Skin: Fair-wheatish, clear, well-groomed
- Height: Approx 5'10" (178 cm)
- Build: Lean-athletic corporate build

### Signature Looks

**Look A – Ego / Brand Phase**
- Expensive dark navy or charcoal Parisian suit (₹45,000)
- Crisp white shirt, luxury tie optional
- Shiny black leather shoes (₹15,000)
- Luxury watch that catches light
- Italian fabric white khadi kurta (ironically expensive) when at centre
- Perfect posture, chin slightly up

**Look B – Transformation Phase**
- Simple plain white cotton kurta (cheap, ordinary)
- Simple white pajama
- No watch, no branded items
- Slightly hunched shoulders initially from shame, then upright with humility
- Eyes softer, moist in final scenes

### Emotional Arc
1. Arrogant superiority
2. Shock & humiliation in meeting
3. Rage + panic in rain
4. Deep shame when helped by Kaka
5. Inner earthquake
6. Tears + genuine respect
7. Humble, soft-spoken, grateful

## 2. Devashish Kaka (The Soul King)

**Age**: 58  
**Occupation**: Municipal corporation sanitation worker (safai karmchari)  
**Daily routine**: Wake 3:30 AM, clean streets & drains

### Physical Appearance (Lock these)
- Face: Weathered, kind, deep wrinkles from sun & work, but glowing with inner peace
- Eyes: Soft, large, full of pure love and zero judgment – permanent gentle smile in eyes
- Hair: Thinning, salt-and-pepper, simple cut, often wet/messy in rain
- Skin: Darker wheatish, sun-tanned, slightly rough
- Build: Lean, wiry strong arms from years of physical labour, slightly bent posture from work
- Hands: Rough, calloused, strong

### Signature Looks

**Look A – Centre / Daily**
- Extremely faded, multiple-times-washed white cotton kurta (almost greyish in places)
- Simple white or faded pajama
- Sits quietly in the back corner

**Look B – Rain / Service Scene**
- Bright yellow raincoat (old, practical)
- Iron rod / tool for clearing manhole
- Body covered in muddy rain water up to chest
- Hair completely wet and sticking
- Still the same soft spiritual smile

### Personality & Aura
- Zero ego
- Sees everyone as “Baba’s child”
- Radiates “Himalaya-like” inner strength even when covered in filth
- Voice: Soft, warm, affectionate (“Mayank bhai”)
- Never takes money for help – pure seva

### Key Visual Metaphor
Even when body is in mud, the **invisible Crown of Light** shines on his head.  
Mayank’s expensive suit becomes dirty while Kaka’s faded kurta remains pure in spirit.

## Consistency Rules for AI Generation
1. Always start with the same face reference images for both characters.
2. Use InstantID / IP-Adapter / Character Reference features.
3. Keep age, skin tone, eye shape, smile type 100% locked.
4. Only change clothing, posture, environment, and emotion intensity.
5. Generate a “character sheet” first (multiple angles) and reuse.
